Propagate — a procedural pixel farm sim

Propagate is a free browser farming simulation played on desktop or tablet. Every farmer is grown procedurally with rolled ability scores and a real personality: they till fields, form bonds, barter goods, invent recipes, hold elections, and stand watch against orc raids — all rendered in pixel art under a CRT shader. Found a human town or an orc war-hoard; your town persists in your browser and remembers itself, greeting you with a "previously on" catch-up when you return. A world that remembers itself.

Made by Ryan Haigh.
