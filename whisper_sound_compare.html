PROPAGATE — WHISPER SOUNDS

Two sounds, judged separately then together. 1 · KEY POP — a tiny pop per typed character (every keystroke jitters its own pitch and length, so hold a key down and listen for the variance). 2 · FARMER VOICE — the reply writes out word by word, each word chirped in the farmer's own animalese; every farmer's pitch is stable (hashed off their seed), orcs sit low and slow. 3 · THE FULL EXCHANGE plays both against each other: type, hit enter, the farmer answers. Click a card to select it — your picks drive sections 1–3 live.
